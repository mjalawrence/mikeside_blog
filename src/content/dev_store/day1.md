                <h2 class="subheader">
                    August 16th
                </h2>
                <p class="journal_entry">{`
        
                    The desperate burst of effort at Otherside was doomed to be a deadend. I had planned to start building there on Saturday but on Thursday night I had noticed signs of the inevitable. With no intention to put money into a lost cause, I moped for the morning wondering what to do and eventually decided to scope out a pedestrianised road 1/2 a mile up river that I'd heard Pat mention in the past.
                
        `}</p>
                <img class="journal_image" src="./src/assets/images/first_build/backfill/backfill_noforms.jpeg"></img>
                
                <p class="journal_entry">{`
        
                    I went down for a reccy, saw the existing retaining wall and the soft tarmac and the road barrier already down there asking to be backfill and decided to send it. I hauled 75 red bricks from Brickside in my car and set up the formers.
                
        `}</p>
                <img class="journal_image" src="./src/assets/images/first_build/backfill/backfill_bricks.jpeg"></img>
                
                <p class="journal_entry">{`
        
                    There were plentiful rocks and hardcore for the small back fill. I had a wire net and some leftover heras fence for rebar. 
                
        `}</p>
                <div class="carousel">

                    <img class="journal_image" src="./src/assets/images/first_build/backfill/backfill_right.jpeg"></img>
                    <img class="journal_image" src="./src/assets/images/first_build/backfill/backfill_front.jpeg"></img>
                    {/* <img class="journal_image" src="./src/assets/images/first_build/backfill/backfill_close.jpeg"></img> */}
                    <img class="journal_image" src="./src/assets/images/first_build/backfill/backfill_left.jpeg"></img>
                </div>
                <p class="journal_entry">{`
        
                    By 6.30pm I had the materials for concrete, water and a shovel and began mixing up on the tarmac.
                
        `}</p>
                <img class="journal_image" src="./src/assets/images/first_build/backfill/backfill_rebar.jpeg"></img>
                
                <p class="journal_entry">{`
        
                    By 8.30pm the shaping was done. 2x ballast2:sharp1:0.5cement mixes and 1x sharp1:10mmgravel1:0.5cement mixes. The ballast mix for the body and the gravel for the coping and the transition. The tarmac already had a lip so there was no need to chip out a recess for the transition concrete.
                
        `}</p>
                
                <div class="carousel">
                    <img class="journal_image" src="./src/assets/images/first_build/conned/con_right_medium2.jpeg"></img>
                    <img class="journal_image" src="./src/assets/images/first_build/conned/con_face.jpeg"></img>
                    <img class="journal_image" src="./src/assets/images/first_build/conned/con_left.jpeg"></img>
                    <img class="journal_image" src="./src/assets/images/first_build/conned/con_right_medium.jpeg"></img>
                    <img class="journal_image" src="./src/assets/images/first_build/conned/con_left_high.jpeg"></img>
                </div>

                <p class="journal_entry">{`
        
                    The coping was shaped with a tool made from a cyclindrical water bottle screwed to a bit of batten for a handle. I'd first used this improvised coping tool on the big QP at Brickside. Although it served me well, its fundamental flaw was its flimsiness and the fact I was afraid it might break mid-build.
                
        `}</p>
                <img class="journal_image" src="./src/assets/images/first_build/conned/coping_left_dark.jpeg"></img>
                
                
                <p class="journal_entry">{`
        
                    The concrete had gone in well and no one who passed by had been offended by me building.<br>The spot was a goer.
                
        `}</p>
