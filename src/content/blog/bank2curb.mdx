        <h2 class="subheader">
            August Bank Holiday Weekend
        </h2>

        <p class="journal_entry">{`
            I set myself the challenge of concreting two obstacles over the Saturday and most of Sunday with backfill preparations made throughout the week. Although I succeeded, ultimately I overstretched myself and Sunday’s concrete suffered.
        `}</p>
            
        <p class="journal_entry">{`
            The design for the bank2curb must have come from Dan Mirza’s repeatedly requesting a “slope/incline” to decked curb combined with inspiration from Paul Carroll bank to curb obstacle at his new spot.
        `}</p>

        <p class="journal_entry">{`
            The curb stones already existed at Brickside and just needed transporting. I can’t say for sure how much each one weighs but I managed to get 3 in my the back of the Seat Ibiza without crushing any part of myself and doing limited damage to the car which made me feel like a world’s strongest man contestant.
        `}</p>
        
        <img class="journal_image" src="../../../public/images/bank2curb_main/curbs_car.jpeg"></img>

        <p class="journal_entry">{`
            At one point when unloading at the other end, I almost tripped backwards with a curb stone leaning against me which would have been a very bad outcome if it had fallen on me.
        `}</p>
        
        <p class="journal_entry">{`
            I had done a mock up of the backfill cross section at brickside to calculate how many red bricks I would need. That came to about 160.
        `}</p>

        `}</p>

        <h2>23rd August</h2>

        <p class="journal_entry">{`
            First job was to establish the foundation. This meant laying a row of 26 red bricks side by side on a bed of ballast and cement mix to try to prevent any movement over time. Then went on the curb stones lined up at the edge of the row of bricks, leaving 10mm between the curbs to fill with a poly resin? later.
        `}</p>

        <div class="carousel">
            <img class="journal_image" src="../../../public/images/bank2curb_main/foundations.jpeg"></img>
            <img class="journal_image" src="../../../public/images/bank2curb_main/foundations2.jpeg"></img>
        </div>
        
        <p class="journal_entry">{`
            Behind the first row of bricks was placed another lengthways row of bricks that would support the next level of backfill bricks. This layer was laid side by side on their sides, requiring about 39 bricks to bring the level up high enough. For the deck/back section, the total red bricks needed to accommodate the 3 curb stones came to 78.
        `}</p>
        
        <p class="journal_entry">{`
            By noon I had the shuttering in place to contain the deck and make for easy screeding and levelling. In the recess I placed a variety of rocks and loose render that had fallen from the walls at the spot, and then on top of that I laid a section of heras fence as rebar.
        `}</p>
        
        <div class="carousel">
            <img class="journal_image" src="../../../public/images/bank2curb_main/deck_backfill.jpeg"></img>
            <img class="journal_image" src="../../../public/images/bank2curb_main/deck_backfill2.jpeg"></img>
        </div>

        <p class="journal_entry">{`
            I imagine I went home for lunch at that point and after my return for the afternoon shift, I had the concrete deck in and level by 3pm.
        `}</p>

        <img class="journal_image" src="../../../public/images/bank2curb_main/deck_conned_top.jpeg"></img>

        <p class="journal_entry">{`
            I pressed on into the afternoon, laying out the remaining red bricks for the bank backfill, then adding the stones and rubble for in between the gaps, got the heras fence rebar ready and chipped out the tarmac to create a lip for the bank transition.
        `}</p>

        <img class="journal_image" src="../../../public/images/bank2curb_main/bank_backfill.jpeg"></img>

        <p class="journal_entry">{`
            The sound of me chiselling into the floor attracted the attention of workers from the reclaimed brick yard on the other side of the wall and they came to see what I was up to and essentially gave me their blessing to carry on.
        `}</p>

        <p class="journal_entry">{`
            Carry on I did and by 8pm the bank was in and had been seen to by the finishing trowel. It been a 12 hour day with 0 assistance other than some passing moral support.
        `}</p>
